I completed a BA in Computer Science (with minors in Engineering and English Literature) at Swarthmore College. During my time there, I was president of the Swarthmore College Computer Society. I grew up in Portland, Oregon, and now live in San Francisco.

We build the sites, systems, and internal tools to deliver the world's most advanced satellite Internet service to multinational corporations, governments, and other entities with unique needs. I've also contributed to a variety of projects on the Starlink app and website and made improvements to our customer-facing AI tooling.
